The Company conducts background checks on all cleaners and maintains strict hiring standards.

Reporting: If you believe an item is missing after a cleaning, please contact us within 7 days of the service. We’ll check with our cleaner, help you look, and investigate right away.

Formal claims: If the item isn’t found, theft claims require a police report, a copy of that report for the Company, and an opportunity for us to investigate. This allows us to file a claim with our bond.

Exclusions:

  • Cash, gift cards, securities, or cryptocurrency

  • Items of sentimental value only, without a documented market value
