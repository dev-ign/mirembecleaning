The meaning behind our name

Mirembe Means Peace.

In Ugandan Bantu languages, Mirembe means “peace” or “tranquility” — a hope for harmony, for one person and for the whole community.

That’s what we want you to feel when you walk through your door: a calm, clean space and one less thing to worry about.

Our story

Built With Care.
Rooted in Family.

Mirembe Cleaning Company was founded by a husband and wife who are homeowners, multifamily real estate investors, and landlords. Between caring for our own home, our family, and our rental properties, we saw how difficult it could be to find cleaning help that was reliable, efficient, and fairly priced.

So we built the kind of cleaning company we wanted for ourselves.

As a woman- and minority-owned family business serving Connecticut and Massachusetts, we treat every home with the same care we’d give our own. Whether it’s a rental between tenants, a busy family’s home, a parent who needs a little extra help, or someone experiencing health or mobility challenges, our goal is to make life a little easier.

When you choose Mirembe, you’re not just hiring a cleaning company. You’re welcoming a family that cares.
