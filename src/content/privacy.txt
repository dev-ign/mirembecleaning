Mirembe Cleaning Company Privacy Policy
Effective date: October 26, 2026
1. Who we are
Mirembe Cleaning Company LLC (“Mirembe,” “we,” or “us”) is a family-owned Connecticut limited liability company providing home and rental property cleaning in Greater Hartford, Connecticut, and Greater Boston, Massachusetts.
This policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect personal information when you visit mirembecleaning.com, join our waitlist, request or book a cleaning, apply to work with us, communicate with us, or receive our services in your home.
2. Information we collect
Information you give us
•     Waitlist: your email address and, if you choose, your ZIP code.
•     Cleaning requests and bookings: your name, email, phone number, service address, home details (such as bedrooms and bathrooms), selected services and add-ons, preferred dates, and any notes you share. Payment details are entered only in our payment provider’s secure fields.
•     Home and service details: information that helps us care for your home, such as entry instructions, keys, alarm or lockbox codes, parking, pets, priorities, surfaces needing special care, allergies or fragrance sensitivities, and, if you arrange cleaning for a loved one, a family contact who receives visit updates.
•     Cleaner applications: your name, email, phone number, city and state, access to a vehicle, cleaning experience, languages spoken, travel distance, and availability.
•     Messages: anything you share when you email, text, call, or message us. Please don’t send sensitive information we don’t need, such as payment card numbers or identity documents.
Information created when we provide services
•     Service records: appointment dates and times, the services provided, notes about your visit, and any quality or damage reports.
•     Photos: our cleaners photograph finished rooms for quality checks and our records. We avoid capturing mail, documents, family photos, house numbers, or other personal items, and we never post photos of your home publicly without your written permission.


Information collected automatically on our website
•     Technical information: like most websites, our hosting and security providers process information such as your network address, browser and device type, pages requested, and the time of each request.
•     Form security: our forms use submission identifiers, timestamps, and security checks to process requests, prevent spam, and avoid duplicate submissions.
•     Optional analytics and advertising: only if you allow it, we measure page visits and certain actions on our site (see Section 8).
Information from other sources
•     Job sites: if you apply through Indeed or a similar site, we receive the application information you submit there.
•     Background checks: for candidates who receive a conditional job offer, we obtain a background check report only after providing a separate disclosure and receiving written authorization.
•     Public reviews: reviews you choose to post about us on Google or other sites.
3. How we use information
•     Respond to cleaning requests, schedule and provide services, and confirm, remind, and update you about appointments
•     Access your home safely and follow your preferences and instructions
•     Process payments, send receipts, and handle billing questions
•     Check and improve the quality of our work, including follow-up after a clean and our clean guarantee
•     Send waitlist updates, booking announcements, and occasional promotions, which you can opt out of anytime
•     Review job applications, schedule interviews and paid trial cleans, and complete hiring steps
•     Protect our website and forms from spam, abuse, and errors
•     With your consent, understand how our website is used and measure our advertising
•     Meet legal, tax, accounting, and insurance requirements, and resolve disputes or claims
4. Bookings and payments
Our booking form is provided by ZenMaid. It may appear on our website or open on a ZenMaid-hosted page, and information you enter is processed by ZenMaid to manage your booking. Payments are processed by Square. We do not see or store your full card number; Square handles payment information under its own security and privacy practices.
Submitting a booking request does not confirm an appointment. We will confirm your date and time by email or text.
5. Your keys, access codes, and home
We know you are trusting us with access to your home. Keys are labeled with a code, never your address, and stored securely when not in use. Access codes and home details are shared only with the owners and the cleaners assigned to your home, and only to provide your service. When you end recurring service, we return your keys and remove access codes from our active records.
6. Emails and texts
By giving us your email address or phone number, you agree to receive messages about your requests and appointments, such as confirmations, reminders, updates, receipts, and requests for feedback.
We may also send occasional promotional messages, such as booking announcements and seasonal offers. You can stop promotional emails anytime by using the unsubscribe link in the email or contacting hello@mirembecleaning.com, and stop texts by replying STOP. Message and data rates may apply. Opting out of promotions does not stop messages about appointments you have booked.
7. Cleaner applicants
We use application information to evaluate experience, availability, transportation, and service-area fit, and to contact applicants about job opportunities. Our application does not ask for a resume, Social Security number, or bank details. We do not ask about criminal history on the initial application.
Applicant information is stored in our application records and an internal spreadsheet that only the owners can access. If you receive a conditional offer, we will provide a separate background check disclosure and authorization form. If you are hired, your employment information is handled under our employee policies.
8. Optional analytics and advertising
If you allow optional tracking, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website and Meta Pixel to measure Facebook and Instagram advertising. These services may process your browser and device information, online identifiers, and the pages and actions described below, under their own privacy policies.
With your consent, we measure page visits, interest in service details, clicks on booking links, views of the booking form, and new waitlist sign-ups. We do not put your name, email address, phone number, service address, or booking notes into the information we send to these services.
If you decline, the analytics and advertising tools are not loaded, and you can still use every form on our site.
9. Cookies, browser storage, and your choices
Optional analytics and advertising tools load only after you choose to allow them. You can change your choice anytime using the “Tracking preferences” link on our website. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a choice to decline optional tracking.
Your choice is saved in your browser. With your consent, we may also temporarily store campaign information, such as which ad brought you to our site. A separate setting remembers if you closed our holiday promotion banner. If you withdraw consent, we stop sending new optional tracking information and clear the related stored data and known analytics cookies, but we cannot undo information already sent.
You can also clear cookies and site data in your browser settings. Clearing data or switching devices may mean you need to make your choice again.
10. Companies that help us operate
We use trusted service providers to run our website and business. They may process your information only to provide their services to us:
•     Vercel: website hosting and processing of form submissions
•     Supabase: secure storage of waitlist and applicant records
•     Resend: confirmation emails and internal notifications
•     Google Workspace and Google Sheets: business email and organizing waitlist and applicant follow-up
•     ZenMaid: booking requests, scheduling, client records, and appointment messages
•     Square: payment processing
•     Cloudflare Turnstile: security checks that help tell people apart from automated spam
•     Mailchimp: sending our email updates and managing unsubscribes
•     Indeed: job postings and applications
•     Checkr: background checks for candidates with a conditional offer
•     Google Analytics and Meta: optional analytics and advertising measurement, only with your consent
11. When we share information
We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information only:
•     With the service providers listed above, to operate our business
•     With the owners and cleaners assigned to your home, to provide your service
•     With our insurance provider or bond company if needed to handle a claim
•     When required by law, such as a valid subpoena or court order, or to protect the safety of our clients, team, or others
•     If our business is sold or merged, with the new owner, who must continue to protect your information
•     With your permission, such as sharing a review or photo you approve
If you allow optional advertising tracking, Meta and Google may receive online identifiers and website activity as described in Section 8. You can decline or withdraw this choice anytime.
12. How long we keep information
We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes in this policy, or as required by law:
•     Waitlist: until you ask to be removed or unsubscribe, or 2 years after our last contact
•     Client records: while you are a client, and for 3 years after your last service, for billing questions, claims, and our records
•     Access codes and keys: removed from active records and returned when recurring service ends
•     Service photos: 12 months after the service, or longer if needed for an open quality or damage claim
•     Payment and accounting records: 7 years, as required for tax purposes
•     Job applications: 2 years after the application if you are not hired. Records for hired team members are kept as required by employment law.
Copies may remain briefly in provider backups before they are deleted on those providers’ schedules.
13. How we protect information
We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, including secure website connections, access limited to the owners and team members who need it, secure storage of keys and access codes, and security protections on our business accounts. No website or storage method is completely secure, so please never email payment card numbers or other sensitive information.
14. Your privacy choices and requests
You can contact us at hello@mirembecleaning.com to:
•     Ask what personal information we have about you
•     Correct inaccurate information
•     Ask us to delete your information
•     Stop promotional emails or texts
•     Change your website tracking preferences
Please include the name and email address or phone number you used with us so we can verify your request. We will respond within 30 days. We may need to keep some information to meet legal, tax, or insurance requirements, and we will tell you if that applies. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state law. If we decline your request, you may reply to ask us to reconsider.
15. Children’s privacy
Our services and job opportunities are for adults 18 and older, and our website is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact hello@mirembecleaning.com and we will delete it. Please share information about children in your home only when it helps us provide a safe service, such as a nap time to work around.
16. Other websites
Our website links to services such as ZenMaid, Square, social media sites, and job sites, which have their own privacy policies. Our tracking preferences apply only to our website. Some of our service providers may process information outside your state or country under their own safeguards.
17. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy as our services change. We will post the updated version on our website with a new effective date. For important changes, we will also notify current clients by email.
